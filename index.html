What is peekbank?

The Peekbank project is an open database storing eye-tracking data on children’s word recognition in a uniform, tabular format. The project brings together data shared by different research groups, spanning 39 datasets with a total of 3537 children tested.

If you want to browse existing data while planning a study or want to contrast the data you collected to preexisting experiments on a low level, check out the documentation! We also have pointers if you are interested in contributing your data to peekbank to make infant research more open 🦝
